# Code of Conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and focus criticism on work rather than people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and sharing another person's private information are prohibited.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces representing the project. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation, with proportionate warnings or bans. Report incidents privately using the repository's private reporting channel; never publish sensitive evidence in an issue. Reports will be handled discreetly, subject to any necessary safety response.
